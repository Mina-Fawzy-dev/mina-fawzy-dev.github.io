Pipeline

I work where the image meets the tooling — the look, the render, and the scripts that get there faster.

  1. 01

    Look-dev & Shading

    Materials, lighting rigs and render setups that survive production: V-Ray and Corona, studio and HDRI lighting, AOV passes for comp.

  2. 02

    Asset & Scene Prep

    Hard-surface models with clean topology, sane UVs, pivots and naming conventions — assets that hand off between people and tools without breaking.

  3. 03

    Scripting & Automation

    Python and MaxScript utilities for batch import, renaming, scene assembly and render submission — the repetitive work removed from the artist's day.

  4. 04

    AI-Assisted Workflows

    ComfyUI graphs for concept exploration, texture variation and look development, wired into the 3D pipeline instead of sitting next to it.

  5. 05

    Render & Post

    Camera framing, passes, denoising and the final composite — advertising-grade stills and animation, delivered ready to publish.

About

I'm Mina, a 3D artist based in Cairo who drifted toward the technical side — the part where a good tool saves a week of clicking.

I started in graphic design and product visualization, then moved deeper into look-dev and rendering for automotive and product CGI. These days I split my time between producing images and building the scripts and workflows behind them: Python and MaxScript for the DCC side, ComfyUI for AI-assisted look development.

I'm looking for a technical artist role or freelance work where art direction, rendering and tooling meet — on a team that cares about both the pixel and the pipeline.

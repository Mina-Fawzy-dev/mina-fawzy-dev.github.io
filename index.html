About

I'm Mina, a 3D artist and graphic designer based in Cairo. I work across the full production line — modeling and hard-surface detail, look-dev, lighting, rendering, post and art direction — for product visualization, automotive CGI and brand campaigns.

I'm moving into technical art: writing Python and MaxScript utilities, automating the repetitive parts of 3D production, and building AI-assisted workflows in ComfyUI. Good art deserves a better pipeline.
